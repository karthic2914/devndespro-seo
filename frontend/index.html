Frequently asked questions

What does the site health score measure?

Site health combines on-page SEO, technical SEO, and content quality checks from your latest audit. Critical issues such as missing H1 headings or very thin pages pull the score down; resolving duplicates, metadata gaps, and crawl errors raises it.

Who is DevnDespro SEO for?

Marketing and growth teams at Nordic companies who need practical SEO and AI visibility insights without juggling multiple disconnected tools. Agencies and in-house SEO owners can track projects per domain and share clear fix priorities with stakeholders.

How is this different from a one-off audit PDF?

Continuous monitoring of keywords, backlinks, and AI citations sits alongside re-runnable site audits, so improvements compound over time instead of becoming a forgotten report. You can verify fixes, watch spammy backlinks, and re-score health after each release.
